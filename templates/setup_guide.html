How to Setup Captchas Step by Step

Stop raid bots with 1-click challenges

Bot Protection
A

Cloudflare Turnstile (Recommended)

100% Free & No Images

Turnstile is invisible to real users and only shows a fast 1-second checkbox. Zero annoying traffic light or motorcycle picture puzzles.

Exact Steps:

  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign in.
  2. Click Turnstile in the left sidebar navigation.
  3. Click the blue Add Widget button.
  4. Give it any widget name (like FreeCord Verify).
  5. Under Domains, add your domain or tunnel host (like ngrok-free.app, loca.lt, or your VPS IP/domain).
  6. Select Managed widget type and click Create.
  7. Copy the Site Key (e.g. 0x4AAAA...) and Secret Key.
  8. Open FreeCord Settings → Security, paste both keys, and toggle Turnstile ON!
B

hCaptcha Challenge

Alternative

hCaptcha requires users to click matching picture tiles. Great for high-threat verification rooms.

Exact Steps:

  1. Create a free account at hcaptcha.com.
  2. Click Sites in the top navigation → New Site.
  3. Add your domain/tunnel address under hostnames.
  4. Set difficulty to Moderate or Easy.
  5. Click Save and copy your Sitekey.
  6. Click your profile icon → Settings → copy your Secret Key.
  7. Open FreeCord Settings → Security, paste both keys, and toggle hCaptcha ON!

Anti-Fraud, Anti-VPN & IPQualityScore Setup

Deep dive into how FreeCord protects your servers

Multi Engine

Do I have Anti-VPN enabled by default?

YES! FreeCord has built-in offline datacenter ASN intelligence active with zero extra configuration. It automatically detects and blocks known VPN providers, datacenter hosting servers (like AWS, Hetzner, OVH, DigitalOcean), and proxy exit nodes.

1

How to connect IPQualityScore (IPQS) or ProxyCheck

If you want commercial grade fraud scoring with 99.9% VPN coverage:

  1. Sign up for a free API key at proxycheck.io or ipqualityscore.com.
  2. Copy your generated API key from their dashboard.
  3. In FreeCord, open Settings → Security.
  4. Paste your key into ProxyCheck / Anti-Fraud API Key and click Save.
  5. FreeCord will now query live threat feeds on every single verification attempt!
2

Discord Snowflake Account Age Filter

Every Discord User ID is a mathematical 64-bit snowflake timestamp. FreeCord decodes the exact millisecond an account was created.

  • Set Min Account Age to 7 or 14 days in Settings.
  • Freshly generated raid alt accounts get stopped instantly at the door.
  • Real members with aged accounts verify in 1 second.

How to Import Existing Tokens

Bring your members over without making them verify again

Direct Restore

If you already have member OAuth2 tokens from an old bot, previous backup file, or database export, you can load them directly into FreeCord. You do not need to ask anyone to re-verify.

Accepted Token Formats

Paste tokens in any of these styles (one per line):

15338949344:vQ8x9a...:rf7B1z...
15338949344:vQ8x9a...
vQ8x9a9s8d7f6g5h4j3k2...
{"user_id": "153389...", "access_token": "..."}

Step by Step Instructions

  1. Open the Migrations page.
  2. Click the purple Import Tokens button at top right.
  3. Pick the bot you want these tokens linked to.
  4. Paste your token list into the box and click Import Tokens.
  5. FreeCord encrypts each token and saves it to your verified list.
  6. Click Start Restore Task to pull them into your server!
9

How to Export Tokens

Download or copy your verified tokens

Data Freedom

You own your data. FreeCord lets you export all your tokens anytime in multiple formats:

  1. Head to the Migrations page.
  2. Click Export Tokens at top right.
  3. Choose the format: ID:Access:Refresh, ID:Access, raw tokens, or full JSON.
  4. Click Download File to save a backup or Copy to paste them wherever you need.
10

Bot Team & Permission Checkmarks

Add helpers and choose what they can do

Team Access

You can add moderators or helpers to your bot with custom permissions:

  1. Go to the Bots page.
  2. Click the Team button on any bot card.
  3. Enter their username and role title (like Helper, Moderator, or Admin).
  4. Checkmark the actions they can perform (Backups, Migrations, Blacklist, Member Info, Security Settings, or Token Export).
  5. Checkmark which Discord servers they can manage (All Servers or specific servers).
  6. Click Save Team Member.

Discord Login for Dashboard Setup

Configure Continue with Discord for signing into FreeCord

Admin Settings
Why is this in Settings instead of Add Bots?

Verification Bots join your Discord servers to assign roles, ban raid bots, and take backups.
Dashboard Discord Login is a separate login gateway that lets you and your team sign into the FreeCord web panel with your Discord accounts. Setting it up in Settings → Discord Login gives you full control.

Exact Setup Steps

  1. Open Settings and scroll to the Discord Login for Dashboard card.
  2. Copy your instance Redirect URL directly from the copy box in Settings.
  3. In the Discord Developer Portal, click your application → OAuth2 → General.
  4. Under Redirects, click Add Redirect, paste your FreeCord URL, and click Save Changes.
  5. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret, paste them into FreeCord Settings, toggle Enable on, and click Save Discord Login Settings.
  6. Go to /login, click Continue with Discord and you will be instantly logged in.

Free Ngrok Domain

Get 1 permanent free domain from ngrok so your URL never changes.

  2. Claim your free static domain

ngrok http --domain=YOUR_DOMAIN.ngrok-free.app 8000
2

Cloudflare Tunnel

Zero port forwarding with automatic free SSL security.

  1. Download cloudflared
  2. Instant quick tunnel:
cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8000

Host 24/7 on Oracle Always Free, DigitalOcean, or Hetzner.

  2. Clone and start:
./run.sh
