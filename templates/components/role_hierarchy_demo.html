Discord Role Hierarchy Rule

Your Bot role must be higher than the @Verified role

Crucial Step
Discord Server Settings → Roles Drag to reorder
👑 Server Owner / Admin
Highest
🤖 Your Bot Role
MUST BE ABOVE
🛡️ @Verified Role
Assigned to users
@everyone
Lowest

Why this is required:

Discord security does not let any bot give a role that sits higher than itself. If your Bot role is below @Verified, Discord will block role assignment with a permission error.

Step 1:

Open Discord Server Settings → Roles

Step 2:

Drag your Bot's role above @Verified

Step 3:

Click Save Changes in Discord!
