Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, straightforward answers to everything about verification, bots, anti-vpn, and server restores.

1 Do I have Anti-VPN enabled by default?

Yes! FreeCord has anti-vpn protection built right in, so it works immediately with no setup needed.

It automatically catches and blocks VPN servers and hosting datacenters like AWS, OVH, DigitalOcean, and Hetzner while letting real members verify without issues. If you want even extra protection, you can also add a free ProxyCheck or IPQualityScore key in Settings.

2 What is the difference between a Bot Token and a Client Secret?

Bot Token: Think of this as your bot's password. It allows FreeCord to log into Discord, create channels, assign roles, and take server backups.

Client Secret: This is the OAuth2 security key. When members verify, Discord gives you a temporary authorization token that expires in 7 days. The Client Secret is what lets FreeCord refresh that token anytime so you can restore members months later.

3 Why do I need a Redirect URL?

When someone clicks your server's verification link, Discord opens an authorization page ("FreeCord wants to access your account").

Once they click "Authorize", Discord needs to know where to send them back. The Redirect URL tells Discord: "Send the verified user back to my FreeCord instance so they get their Verified role!"

4 Is FreeCord really 100% free with unlimited members?

Yes, completely free forever. Other services like RestoreCord block you with a paywall after just 25 members.

FreeCord is self-hosted on your own machine or VPS with zero limits, zero paywalls, and zero restrictions. You can verify and restore tens of thousands of members without paying a dime.

5 Can I restore my server if the original server gets deleted or banned?

Yes, that is exactly why FreeCord exists.

1. Take a server backup from the Backups page (saves all roles, categories, channels, permissions, and emojis into a JSON file).
2. If your server is lost, create a fresh Discord server, invite your bot, and click Restore Backup.
3. Then go to Migrations, select the new server, and click Start Restore to pull all your verified members back into the new server automatically!

6 How do I make my verification link work on mobile and for other people?

If you run FreeCord on your local PC, http://localhost:8000 only works on your own computer.

To let members verify from anywhere in the world, use a free tunnel (like Ngrok or Cloudflare Tunnel) or host FreeCord on a cheap VPS (like Oracle Always Free, DigitalOcean, or Hetzner). Check the Setup Guide for exact 1-minute commands.

7 What details does the "Inspect Full Information" button show?

Clicking the blue Inspect button on any member reveals everything grababble about that user:

  • Discord Identity: User ID, username, avatar URL, email, and Discord account age (days old).
  • Network & Location: IP address, country, region, city, zip code, ISP provider, and ASN.
  • Device Details: Operating system, browser family, device model, and screen resolution.
  • Behavior History: Number of times they left servers and verification timestamp.

8 Why does Discord have rate limits when restoring members?

Discord allows bots to add around 1 member per second to avoid crashing servers. If you blast 500 requests at once, Discord will pause the bot with a 429 Too Many Requests response.

FreeCord uses smart pacing with automatic retry backoff and proxy rotation so your migrations complete safely without getting blocked.

9 What happens if someone uses a VPN to verify?

If Anti-VPN is enabled, the verification page immediately rejects the connection: "VPN or proxy detected. Please disable your VPN to verify."

An alert is also recorded in your audit logs showing the user's ID, flagged IP, and detected VPN network.

10 How are bot tokens and member tokens secured?

All sensitive credentials (bot tokens, client secrets, and member access/refresh tokens) are encrypted with Fernet AES-128 CBC encryption before being saved into your local database.

Even if someone gets a copy of your database file, they cannot read any tokens without the unique encryption key in your .env file.

11 What does Import Tokens mean and when should I use it?

Import Tokens lets you load members you already have into FreeCord without making them re-verify.

If you have tokens from previous backups, old bots, or database exports, you can paste them all at once in the Migrations tab by clicking Import Tokens.

FreeCord checks each token, encrypts it on your machine, and links it to your bot. After importing, you can immediately pull those members into any server you own.

12 How do I export my verified tokens?

You can export all verified tokens anytime in one click.

Go to Migrations → click Export Tokens. You can choose whether to download as a text file or JSON, filter by bot or active members, and copy the decrypted tokens directly.

13 Can I add helpers or moderators to manage my bot?

Yes, you can add team members with custom permissions.

On the Bots page, click the Team button on any bot. You can add a collaborator and checkmark exactly what they are allowed to do (like taking backups, running migrations, or viewing member IPs) and pick which servers they can manage.

14 How does "Continue with Discord" dashboard login work?

It lets you and your team sign into FreeCord using your Discord accounts in one click.

When you click "Continue with Discord", Discord verifies your identity securely and returns your profile to FreeCord. If it is your first time logging in as the server owner, you receive full admin access automatically. It also shows your real Discord avatar and username in the dashboard footer!

15 Why is Discord Login configured separately from verification bots in Settings?

Because they serve completely different purposes.

Verification Bots: Are bots that you invite into Discord servers to give verified roles, ban raid bots, and snapshot backups.
Discord Dashboard Login: Is an authentication gateway for you and your staff to log into the web dashboard. By keeping it in Settings → Discord Login, you can use any dedicated Discord app or master credentials without messing with your server bots.

16 What should I do if Discord says "Invalid OAuth2 redirect_uri"?

Discord blocks OAuth2 logins if your URL is not registered in the Developer Portal.

1. Open the Settings page in FreeCord and copy the Redirect URL (e.g. https://your-url.com/api/oauth/callback).
2. Go to the Discord Developer Portal → click your Application → OAuth2 → General.
3. Under Redirects, click Add Redirect, paste the link, and click Save Changes.
4. Click "Continue with Discord" again and it will work immediately!

17 Can my staff and moderators log in with their Discord accounts too?

Yes!

Once you enable Discord Login in Settings, any staff member or bot collaborator can click "Continue with Discord" on the login screen to sign in with their Discord account.
